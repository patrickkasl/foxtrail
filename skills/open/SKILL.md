name: open
description: Opens the Fox Trail page in the maker's browser. It works when the maker types /foxtrail:open in Claude Code; do not start it on your own.
compatibility: Claude Code, with Node.js 18 or newer on the computer.
---

The maker asked to see the fox trail.

When the maker types `/foxtrail:open` in Claude Code, Fox Trail opens its page in their browser by itself, before you read this, and tells you how that went in a line that starts with "Fox Trail outcome:".

If you were given such a line: reply with the sentence after "Fox Trail outcome:", word for word, and nothing else.

If you were not given such a line, the page was not opened. Then reply with only this: "Type /foxtrail:open yourself and the trail opens in your browser. Fox Trail runs in Claude Code and needs Node.js on your computer."

Either way, do not run a command and do not open anything yourself.
